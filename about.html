Welcome to Olivet

The church where everybody
is somebody in Christ alone!

Founded on 24 October 1971, Olivet Baptist Church, Olivet Heights, Oyo, is a community of Bible-believing Christians affiliated with the Nigerian Baptist Convention.

We worship God, proclaim the Gospel of Jesus Christ, and seek to transform lives through the power of God’s Word. Through worship, biblical teaching, prayer, discipleship, fellowship, missions, and community outreach, we want people to find a spiritual home and grow in faith.

As part of the Baptist family, we uphold the authority of the Holy Scriptures, the lordship of Jesus Christ, personal salvation, believer’s baptism, and the calling to share the Good News.
