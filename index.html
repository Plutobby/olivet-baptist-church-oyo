Rooted in faith since 1971

More than a building.
A family in Christ.

Founded on 24 October 1971, Olivet Baptist Church, Olivet Heights, is part of the Nigerian Baptist Convention. For generations, we’ve gathered to worship, learn God’s Word, encourage one another, and serve our community.

Our heart is simple: make everybody somebody in the love of Christ.
