Before you arrive

A little more
about your visit.

We want your first visit to feel easy. If there’s anything else you’d like to know, we’re happy to help.

Talk to us ↗
What time is Sunday worship?+

Sunday worship begins at 10:30 am. You’re also welcome to join us earlier for prayer, Sunday School, and Disciples’ Lifestyle.

Where is the church?+

We’re at Olivet Heights, Oyo, Nigeria. Get directions on Google Maps.

Can I come if I’m new to church?+

Yes. Whether you’re exploring faith or looking for a church family, you’re welcome to join us.

How can I find out about ministries?+

Ask us when you visit, or call 0806 760 1307 to learn about opportunities for children, youth, families, and serving.

What about children, accessibility, or parking?+

We’d be glad to help you prepare. Please call the church to ask about children’s activities, accessibility, parking, service language, or anything else you need to know.
