Community · May 21, 2026

Fostering community at
Olivet Baptist Church.

A church is more than a place of worship. It’s a community where people grow spiritually and support one another.

A community of faith

Olivet Baptist Church is more than just a place of worship; it is a vibrant community where individuals come together to grow spiritually and support one another. We aim to create an environment that feels welcoming and accessible for everyone.

Engaging community life

From weekly worship services to special programs and outreach initiatives, there are ways for people to take part and connect. We welcome church members and newcomers to share in worship, fellowship, and the life of our church.

Share, connect, and grow

The heart of Olivet Baptist Church lies in sharing faith and connecting with the community. Members are encouraged to participate in ministries, share testimonies, and engage with one another through church activities. There are many ways to find belonging and continue growing.

Our mission is to show the love of God through service, fellowship, and spiritual enrichment. We invite you to be part of this church family.
